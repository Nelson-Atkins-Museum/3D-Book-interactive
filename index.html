Breaking News!

Thai is alive and thriving at Houston Zoo

After Thai’s escape from Morgan Berry’s farm, he was relocated to the Houston Zoo in 1980. In 2025, he celebrated his 60th birthday, making him the second oldest male Asian elephant in North America.

LEFT: Thai with a zookeeper in 1980 when he arrived in Houston. Photo courtesy of Houston Zoo. RIGHT: Thai pictured in 2017. Photo courtesy of Houston Zoo.
